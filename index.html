What is brazing?

🔥
Definition — CSA B51 & ASME Section IX
"A group of metal-joining processes that produce a coalescence of materials by heating them to a suitable temperature and by using a filler metal having a liquidus above 450 °C (840 °F) and below the solidus of the base materials. The filler metal is distributed between the closely fitted surfaces of the joint by capillary action."
When performed on regulated equipment (refrigeration systems, pressure piping, pressure vessels), brazing constitutes regulated work under the Safety Standards Act, SBC 2003, c.39.

A Brazing Procedure Specification (BPS) is a written document defining how a brazed joint will be produced. It must conform to ASME Section IX and be registered with TSBC before use on regulated equipment.

Minimum BPS contents (ASME Sec. IX)

✔ Procedure name/number & revision
✔ Organization (procedure developer) name
✔ Brazing process type
✔ All essential & non-essential variables
Suggested form: ASME Section IX Form QB-482

Qualification testing methods (QB-202)

1
Tension test
Determines ultimate strength of brazed butt, scarf, lap, and rabbet joints.
QB-150 series
2
Guided bend test
Determines soundness and ductility of butt and scarf joints.
QB-160 series
3
Peel test
Determines bond quality and quantity of defects in lap joints.
QB-170 series
4
Sectioning test
Determines soundness of workmanship coupons. Substitutes for peel test when impractical.
QB-180 series
5
Visual examination
Inspects external surface for filler metal continuity, size, contour, and wetting of fillet.
QB-190 series
Brazing performed for procedure qualification may also satisfy performance qualification for the brazer if all requirements of ASME Sec. IX Article XIII are met.

Only a qualified individual working under an appropriate class of contractor licence may perform brazing of regulated pressure equipment — even though anyone can obtain a brazer performance qualification per ASME Section IX.

Qualification process

1
Braze a test coupon
In strict accordance with the developed or adopted BPS. Testing may be conducted by a licensed contractor or at a Recognized Test Administrator (RTA) facility.
ASME Sec. IX Article XIII · QB-302
2
Destructive or NDE testing
Test coupon evaluated to determine joint integrity. Results documented on BPQ form.
QB-302 — testing requirements
3
Complete BPQ record
Document brazer name, employer, BPS used, date, test results. Retain and make available to TSBC safety officer on inspection.
ASME Form QB-484
4
Maintain continuity
Brazer must use each qualified process at least once every 6 months. Field work, repair, alteration, or shop brazing for continuity maintenance all qualify as evidence.
QB-320 continuity · QB-322 requalification

Portability of qualifications — transferring to a new employer

A brazer's performance qualification may be accepted by a new employer/contractor provided all four conditions are met:
1. New employer obtains a copy of the BPQ (Form QB-484) showing the brazer's name, qualifying employer, and date.
2. New employer obtains continuity records showing each relevant BPS was used at no more than 6-month intervals from qualification to transfer date.
3. New employer verifies essential variables on brazer's existing BPQ are within the limits of the new employer's BPS.
4. New employer formally accepts the brazer's performance qualification record within their QCP.

A BPS must be registered with Technical Safety BC under s.78 of the Power Engineers, Boiler, Pressure Vessel and Refrigeration Safety Regulation BEFORE use on any regulated pressure equipment.

Registration options

🏢
Individual contractor registration
A licensed contractor develops, qualifies, and registers their own BPS directly with TSBC. Contractor is the procedure owner and responsible party.
🤝
Group / association registration
A larger representative group (industry association, educational institution, cooperative) registers BPS for use by members. Contractors must formally adopt the procedure per requirements below.

Adopting a registered BPS from another organization

All four conditions must be met before adopting a group-registered BPS:
1
ASME compliance verified
The BPS fully meets applicable requirements of ASME Section IX.
2
TSBC registration confirmed
The BPS is registered with Technical Safety BC (confirm Admin # from registrant).
3
Compatibility verified
Adopting contractor verifies procedure is compatible with the brazing work they intend to perform and meets applicable codes of construction (B31.5, CSA B52, etc.).
4
QCP documentation updated
Contractor's QCP addresses the adoption and includes a copy or reference to the registered BPS and associated documents.
